Oil Crosses $100 as Iran Conflict Chokes Gulf Shipping

Source: The Wall Street Journal / Reuters | Relevance: Commodities, Supply Chain, Geopolitics

Traders now treat every diplomatic headline as a volatility event, and forward curves are pricing sustained uncertainty rather than a clean resolution.

A sustained move above $95 flows directly into freight contracts, cold-chain costs, and packaging resin within one to two quarters. Worth locking forward freight where terms allow, and stress-testing landed-cost models against a $110 scenario.
